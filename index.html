Questions before installing?

Is Pullhub free?

Yes. You can start free. Pro is built for heavier workflows that need more room for active projects and more export and sharing usage.

Does Pullhub work outside Chrome?

Chrome is the officially supported browser. Pullhub should also work in many Chromium-based browsers such as Edge, Brave, Arc, and Comet, though some features and sign-in may depend on the browser.

Does Pullhub work with Google Slides?

Yes. Pullhub collects references from the web and pushes them into Google Slides.

Where are my boards saved?

Your boards are saved privately in your browser — nothing sits on our servers in v1. Export a ZIP anytime as a backup.

Can I share boards?

Yes — public board sharing links are part of Pro. In v1, share links are snapshot-based, so re-share when you want to update the public version.

Why does Pullhub need Google access?

Pullhub uses Google access to put your images into Slides. Your other Google Drive files stay in your account.
