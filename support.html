Quick fixes

Slides & pushing

Google Slides isn't detected

  • Make sure a Google Slides file is open.
  • Refresh the Google Slides tab.
  • Open Pullhub again from the toolbar.
  • Restart Chrome if it still is not detected.

Images are missing or aren't pushed to Slides

  • Confirm you are signed into Google and the target Slides file is open.
  • Try pushing fewer images first.
  • If a site blocks an image, Pullhub tries to save a copy to your Google Drive so Slides can display it.
  • If an image is still missing, push those images again, or capture them with screenshot capture and push again.

Account & billing

Google says “This app hasn't been verified”

  • This warning can appear while Pullhub is going through Google's app review.
  • It does not mean Pullhub is unsafe.
  • Only continue if you installed Pullhub from the official Pullhub source.
  • To continue, click Advanced, then Go to Pullhub. Google may show the button as “Go to Pullhub (unsafe)” during this review period.
  • Your Google files stay in your account; Pullhub only uses access to push images to Slides.

Switching or signing out of a Google account

  • If you use multiple Google accounts, make sure Chrome, Google Slides, and Pullhub are using the same account.
  • Sign out and sign in again if Pullhub seems connected to the wrong account.

Paid but Pro didn't unlock

  • Reopen Pullhub and wait a moment for the license check to refresh.
  • Restart Chrome if Pro still does not appear.
  • Make sure you are using the same email you used for payment.
  • If it still does not unlock, email support with your payment email and approximate purchase time.

Sharing

A public link is not updating

  • A share link captures your board as it looks when you share it.
  • Images you add later will not appear until you disable the link and share again.

A shared board says "unavailable"

  • The board may be private, disabled, deleted, or the link may no longer be valid.
  • If it is your board, open Pullhub and turn Public share back on to create a fresh link.
  • If someone sent you the link, ask the board owner to enable sharing again or send a new link.

Still stuck? Email support with a screenshot.

Saving & export

Right-click "Save to Pullhub" is missing or images won't save

  • Reload the page after installing or updating Pullhub.
  • The menu usually appears on regular webpage images, not on browser or system pages.
  • Some Chrome pages, PDF pages, and restricted websites may block extensions.
  • Try screenshot capture if a page blocks normal image saving.

ZIP export is missing some images

  • Pullhub exports captured and downloadable images.
  • Some websites block image downloads, so blocked images may be saved as web links instead of picture files.
  • You can find those links in metadata.json inside the ZIP.
  • To keep blocked images as picture files next time, capture them with screenshot capture first, then export.

My boards disappeared

  • Pullhub boards are saved locally in your browser in v1.
  • Clearing browser data or uninstalling the extension may remove local boards.
  • If you exported a ZIP backup, import that ZIP to restore your board.
  • If you did not export a ZIP backup, local boards usually cannot be recovered.

Still stuck? Email support with a screenshot.

What is the “Pullhub Uploads” folder in my Drive?

  • Pullhub may create this folder when a website blocks an image you push to Slides.
  • Pullhub only uploads copies of images you push to Slides, not your other Drive files.
  • These image copies may be shared by link so Google Slides can display them.
  • You can delete the folder or files anytime, but older Slides may stop showing those images.
